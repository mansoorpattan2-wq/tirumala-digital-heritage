# Tirumala Digital Heritage: editorial research and asset register

Reviewed 25 October 2026. An independent educational project, not an official TTD service. The research establishes a concise foundation, not an archaeological survey. Describe the 3D environment as a stylized interpretation, never a measured reconstruction. Do not imply access to restricted interiors.

## Verified chapter notes

1. **Sacred Seven Hills — landscape and tradition.** Tirumala's sacred landscape is associated with seven hills. In temple tradition, Adisesha becomes the Seshadri range and Venkatadri is the abode of Sri Venkateswara. Attribute that account to devotional tradition, not geological history. Source: [TTD Temple Legend](https://www.tirumala.org/TempleLegend.aspx). Avoid a numeric age or elevation without a specialist source.
2. **Temple Exterior Reveal — built heritage.** TTD describes a complex developed by successive generations. The official account lists its area as 16.2 acres. Source: [TTD Temple History](https://www.tirumala.org/TTDTempleHistory.aspx).
3. **Maha Dwaram — a layered threshold.** TTD dates the entrance's successive enlargement to the thirteenth century onward and describes its current height as fifty feet. Source: [TTD Temple History](https://www.tirumala.org/TTDTempleHistory.aspx).
4. **Royal Patronage — recorded history.** Temple inscriptions preserve patronage from Pallava, Chola, Pandya and Vijayanagara rulers across centuries. Use broad periods instead of inventing a single founding date. Source: [TTD, History of Tirupati, Volume I, foreword](https://ebooks.tirumala.org/downloads/history_of_tirupati_vol_i.pdf).
5. **Mandapams and Traditions — living architecture.** Pillared pavilions combine sculptural craft and ceremonial use. Krishnaraya Mandapam holds copper statues of Krishnadevaraya and his consorts; TTD records seven visits by the emperor. Tirumalaraya Mandapam developed in phases. Source: [TTD News, Mandapams in Tirumala, 21 September 2024](https://news.tirumala.org/mandapams-in-tirumala/).
6. **Ananda Nilaya Vimanam — the gilded roof.** The gold-covered structure above the sanctum has a history of renewal. TTD describes twentieth-century work and a consecration ceremony in 1964. Its name is rendered as Abode of Happiness. Source: [TTD News, Ananda Nilayam, 18 September 2019](https://news.tirumala.org/ananda-nilayam-the-heaven-on-earth/). Exterior interpretation only.
7. **Living Heritage and Festivals — ongoing devotion.** Brahmotsavams is a nine-day celebration with processions on symbolic vehicles. The attribution of its origin to Brahma is temple tradition. Source: [TTD Utsavams](https://www.tirumala.org/Utsavams.aspx), [TTD Brahmotsavam sequence](https://www.tirumala.org/Bramhostavam.aspx). Garuda Seva belongs to the fifth day; Rathotsavam to the eighth; Chakra Snanam to the ninth. Treat this as an educational sequence, not a live calendar.
8. **Pilgrimage Preparation — official onward links.** Arrange travel and accommodation in advance, follow queues and local directions, and respect worship. Source: [TTD Do's and Don'ts](https://www.tirumala.org/Dos_Donot.aspx). Current reservations belong on the [official TTD portal](https://ttdevasthanams.ap.gov.in/).

## Practical visitor sources

- Travel: [TTD, To Reach Tirupati](https://www.tirumala.org/ToReachTirupati.aspx) confirms road, rail and air connections. Do not reproduce fixed journey durations as promises.
- Accommodation: [TTD, Accommodation at Tirumala](https://www.tirumala.org/AccommodationAtTirumala.aspx) describes guest-house areas, choultries and dormitory halls. Availability must be checked with TTD.
- Reservations: [TTD Advance Booking](https://www.tirumala.org/AdvanceBooking.aspx) links the official portal. Its booking-window details differ from its FAQ; therefore do not hardcode eligibility windows, fees or availability.
- General temple guidance: [TTD Do's and Don'ts](https://www.tirumala.org/Dos_Donot.aspx). Link official instructions rather than presenting the project as a source of operational authority.
- Darshan categories: the official navigation identifies Sarvadarshan, Special Entry Darshan, Divya Darshan and facilities for aged/disabled pilgrims. Rules may change around events; link to TTD for eligibility and arrangements.
- Services: the official website links Annaprasadam, medical services, accommodation and local transport. Do not display invented live queue data.

## Editorial cautions and uncertainty

- The TTD history page includes religious belief alongside construction history. Its statement about a five-thousand-year divine abode is not a verified archaeological founding date.
- TTD pages differ on some historic dates and pillar counts. Do not reproduce uncertain numbers in the 3D scene. The model should not be described as a blueprint or surveyed plan.
- The exact dating of Queen Samavai's gift is inconsistent in wider literature. Omit a hardcoded date pending epigraphic review.
- Architecture in photographs is evidence for visual atmosphere; it is insufficient for reconstructing hidden layouts.
- No accurate, permission-cleared temple GLB was verified. The app should transparently use a stylized model.
- No TTD logos or TTD-owned photographs were copied. The included photos are from named Wikimedia contributors with explicit reuse licenses.
- A filename search returned Tirumala hill in Wanaparthy, a different location. That image was rejected.

## Design interpretation

Use the actual white entrance gopuram as the visual anchor, with a smaller golden vimanam behind it. Forested ridgelines, warm stone, ivory sculpture and antique gold are grounded in the exterior photographs. The 3D scene may abstract those materials and proportions as long as the simplified status is visible. A calm, reverent museum presentation is more appropriate to the brief than comedy or sensationalism.
