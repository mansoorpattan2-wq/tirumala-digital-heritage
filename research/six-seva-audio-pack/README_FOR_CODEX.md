# Six-Seva Original Ambient Sound Pack

Six original synthesized instrumental cues, 16 seconds each, WAV mono 22050Hz. Not chants, live recordings, official TTD music or reproductions of historical ritual performances.

Copy audio files into your project `public/audio/sevas/` (or equivalent) and integrate by filename according to `audio_manifest.json`. Keep the shrine's two existing recordings unchanged. Do not autoplay before user gesture. Add mute, volume, pause, captions (music-only descriptions), and scene transitions. Loop only if desired, with a crossfade to avoid silence at joins.

For authentic seva recitation, use a separately licensed and verified recording. Do not generate purported Sanskrit mantras. Test filenames, browser playback, volume balance and mobile support.
