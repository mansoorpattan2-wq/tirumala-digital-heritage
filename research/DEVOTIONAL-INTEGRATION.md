# Devotional artwork and motion

The project owner's two supplied portraits are retained in `public/assets/devotional/` with 640px and 1024px WebP derivatives. Both are available in the gallery and the separate `/darshan` experience. The twilight portrait leads the home page. The original seven-scene exterior pilgrimage and optional festival preserve their order.

The owner's later request explicitly adds an imagined shrine and harathi, extending the earlier master prompt. This scene is labeled as artistic devotional imagery; it does not claim to show the actual Tirumala sanctum, verified procedures, or live darshan. Door motion, subtle pointer depth, light, incense, and a twelve-second priest foreground sequence provide atmosphere. No deity facial animation or ritual audio recording is used.

Priest layer: generated with the built-in imagegen tool, inspected, retained as `public/assets/devotional/harathi-priest.png`, optimized to `harathi-priest.webp` with alpha preserved. Final prompt:

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: transparent foreground cutout for an artistic devotional web scene. Primary request: one South Indian Hindu temple priest seen entirely from the back and slight left three-quarter view, reverently performing harathi toward an unseen shrine. Full torso and waist down to upper thigh visible. Priest wears a natural ivory cotton dhoti with narrow muted maroon border, bare upper back with sacred thread, dark hair tied in a small traditional knot. His right arm extends forward to the upper right holding a small brass multi-wick arati lamp with five small warm natural flames, realistic anatomically correct hands. Warm low amber lamplight grazes his right shoulder, dark natural shadows, realistic skin and cloth texture, cinematic photograph quality, subdued exposure suitable for a dark temple foreground. Composition: man occupies left third of square composition, hand and lamp extend towards center-right. Entire priest, hand, lamp and flames within frame, no clipped edges. Backdrop: genuinely transparent alpha background, no room, no shrine, no deity, no floor, no text or watermark. This is an artistic illustration, not a depiction of a verified real temple ritual. Avoid exaggerated smoke, bright bloom, unnatural anatomy or cartoon styling.

Motion pauses when the document is hidden and can be paused explicitly. Reduced-motion visitors get still views and instant door transitions. A fixed pool of pointer sparks follows mouse movement and touch drags, fades after movement stops, never intercepts interaction, and disables for reduced motion. Cursor inspiration: Framer custom cursor Follow patterns, https://www.framer.com/updates/custom-cursors ; accessibility reference: https://help.webflow.com/hc/en-us/articles/33961403269907-Troubleshoot-smooth-scroll-and-reduced-motion .

Asset repair: the supplied `06-temple-day-1280.webp` was zero bytes. It was regenerated from its retained original PNG with Sharp (1280 × 720, WebP quality 84). All original scenes and their derivatives decode in the asset checks.

Local launch: `npm run dev` for development; `npm run build` then `npm start` for the static production preview. This integration does not publish the site.
